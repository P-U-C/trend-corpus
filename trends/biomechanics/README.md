# Biomechanics

Public-market exposure to the engineering of human movement.

Four sub-stacks make up the theme:

1. **Robotic orthopedics** — robot-assisted joint reconstruction and spine
   surgery, where the robot is an installed-base / implant-pull-through engine
   (Stryker Mako, Zimmer Biomet ROSA, Globus Medical ExcelsiusGPS).
2. **Exoskeletons / exosuits** — medical (rehab, personal mobility) and
   industrial lift-assist hardware moving from pilots to reimbursed or deployed
   units (Ekso Bionics, Lifeward/ReWalk, Myomo, Wandercraft, German Bionic).
3. **MSK digital health** — software-led musculoskeletal care and motion
   tracking (Hinge Health, Sword Health).
4. **Prosthetics / bionics + personalization** — bionic limbs and personalized,
   3D-printed orthopedic hardware (Ottobock, Materialise), plus the wearable
   biomechanics layer (Garmin).

See `trend.yaml` for scope and the public-safety boundary. Sources, claims,
entities, theses, the decision packet, and the watchlist build the
watchlist-candidate case; `opportunity-config.yaml` tunes the opportunity
generator.
